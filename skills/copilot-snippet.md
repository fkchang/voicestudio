## Text-to-speech / voiceover
For TTS, narration, voiceover or "in my voice", run `~/.local/bin/speak "text" --out /abs/out.wav` (your cloned voice via local VoiceStudio.app). Also `--file`, `--batch script.txt --out-dir DIR` (per-segment timings in manifest.json), `speak voices`. Guide: ~/work/voicestudio/docs/for_llms.md
